About ConvertKit

A small, free toolkit of file converters that respect your privacy by never touching a server.

Why this exists

Most “free online converter” sites upload your document to a server you can't see, keep it for an unknown amount of time, and often ask you to create an account. For a passport scan, an invoice or a client contract, that is a real risk.

ConvertKit takes the opposite approach. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using open-source JavaScript libraries. Your files are read, converted and saved on your own device. There is no backend to upload to, so there is nothing to leak.

What you can do here

  • PDF tools — merge, split, rotate, extract text, and convert to and from images.
  • Image tools — convert between PNG, JPG and WebP, and compress or resize photos.
  • Data tools — convert between CSV, JSON and Excel formats.
  • Document tools — turn Word files into HTML and write Markdown with a live preview.

How it is built

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, no framework and no tracking. Well-known libraries such as pdf-lib, pdf.js, SheetJS, PapaParse, mammoth and marked are pinned to exact versions and served from this site, so the tools keep working even if a CDN goes down. Each one is listed in the third-party licences file.

What it does not do

Because everything runs in your browser, some jobs are out of scope. Accurate PDF-to-Word conversion, OCR for scanned documents, and video or audio conversion all need heavy server-side software. Rather than offer a poor version, ConvertKit leaves them out and focuses on doing the client-side tools well.
