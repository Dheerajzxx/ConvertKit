Frequently asked questions

        

          

            Which spreadsheet formats are supported?
            
Modern .xlsx workbooks, older .xls files and plain .csv files. Each sheet can be exported separately.

          

          

            What happens to formulas and merged cells?
            
Formulas become their calculated values. Merged cells keep the value in the top-left cell while the others export empty.

          

          

            Is my spreadsheet uploaded?
            
No. The workbook is read and converted in the browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server.
