How to compress an image

  1. Add a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Lower the quality slider for a smaller file, and set a maximum width to resize.
  3. Select Compress image.
  4. Drag the handle across the comparison to check quality, then download.

Keep a copy of the original — canvas compression is lossy for JPG and WebP and cannot be undone.

Frequently asked questions

How much smaller will my image get?

It varies by photo. Photos usually shrink the most; screenshots and graphics shrink less. The stats show the exact percentage saved each time.

Does compressing remove EXIF data?

Yes. Drawing the image to a canvas and re-encoding it drops EXIF metadata such as camera model and GPS location, which is often a privacy benefit.

Will the image look different?

At 80–90% quality most photos look identical to the original. Use the before/after slider to compare before you download.
