How to rotate a PDF

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Choose the rotation angle and whether it applies to all or selected pages.
  3. Select Rotate PDF.
  4. Download the corrected document.

Rotations build on the page's current angle, so you can apply it more than once to fine-tune a crooked scan.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate only some pages?

Yes. Pick Selected pages and enter a range such as 2, 4-6.

How much can I rotate a page?

In 90° steps. Applying rotation again adds to the current angle, so two 90° passes equal 180°.

Is the PDF uploaded?

No. Everything is processed locally and the new file is saved straight to your device.
