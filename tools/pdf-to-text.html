Frequently asked questions

        

          

            Why did my PDF return no text?
            
Scanned PDFs are images of pages, so there is no embedded text to read. You would need an OCR service to convert the image to text first.

          

          

            Does the extraction keep the layout?
            
It preserves line breaks and page order, but columns, tables and spacing are approximate. PDFs do not store text as tidy paragraphs.

          

          

            Is my PDF uploaded?
            
No. All extraction happens in your browser tab and the text stays on your device.
