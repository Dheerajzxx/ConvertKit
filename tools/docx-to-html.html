Frequently asked questions

        

          

            Which Word formats work?
            
Modern .docx files only. For older .doc files, open them in Word or Google Docs, save as .docx, then convert.

          

          

            Will the layout look exactly the same?
            
Headings, lists, links, tables and images are preserved, but text boxes, multi-column layouts and exact fonts are simplified.

          

          

            Is the document uploaded?
            
No. The file is unpacked and read in your browser. The HTML is sanitised and rendered inside a sandboxed frame for safety.
