How to convert image formats

  1. Add one or more PNG, JPG or WebP images.
  2. Choose the output format. For JPG and WebP, set the quality slider.
  3. If converting to JPG, pick a background colour for any transparent areas.
  4. Select Convert images and download the result or ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Which formats can I convert between?

PNG, JPG and WebP, in any direction. All current browsers support WebP output.

What happens when I convert a transparent PNG to JPG?

JPG cannot store transparency, so you choose a background colour that fills the transparent pixels. White is the usual choice.

Can I convert several images at once?

Yes. Add as many images as you need; the converted files are packaged into a single ZIP for download.
